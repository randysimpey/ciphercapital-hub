# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 14, 2026*

SecurityAuditAI and the CipherCapital website are operated by **ICT Support By Randy**, a sole proprietorship (eenmanszaak) based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands. This page explains what data we collect, why, and how it's handled.

## Code you submit for scanning

When you paste a public GitHub repository URL for a free scan:

- The repository is cloned into a temporary directory on our scanning infrastructure.
- The scan runs (Gitleaks for secrets, Trivy for dependencies and infrastructure-as-code).
- The temporary clone is **deleted immediately after the scan completes** — it is not stored, backed up, or reused.
- We do not read, review, or manually inspect your code. The process is automated.

This applies to Free, Pro, and Business plans alike. Paid plans add private-repo access and CI/CD integration, not longer code retention.

## Information we collect

- **Email address**, when you request a scan report, sign up for the checklist, or create an account for a paid plan.
- **Scan metadata**: the repository URL you submitted, the timestamp, and the summary results of the scan (counts and categories of findings — not the underlying code).
- **Payment information**, handled entirely by Stripe when you subscribe to Pro or Business. We do not store your card details ourselves.
- **Basic analytics** about site usage (pages visited, general location by country/region), if analytics tooling is enabled on this site.

## How we use this information

- To send you your scan report by email.
- To operate and bill your subscription, if you're on a paid plan.
- To send occasional updates about new tutorials or product changes, only if you opted in (you can unsubscribe at any time).
- We do not sell your data to third parties.

## Third-party services

We rely on a small number of external providers to run this service:

- **Stripe** — payment processing (Pro/Business plans).
- **Gitleaks** and **Trivy** — open-source scanning engines, run on our own infrastructure (not sent to a third party).
- **Email delivery provider** — to send scan reports and opt-in updates.

## Your rights

As we operate from the Netherlands and serve users across the EU, you have the right to:

- Request a copy of any personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction or deletion of that data.
- Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time (unsubscribe link in every email).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at <secrapports@outlook.com>.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Material changes will be communicated to active subscribers by email.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach out at <secrapports@outlook.com>.
