LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacybeleid

Laatst bijgewerkt: 14 september 2026

Dit beleid legt uit hoe CipherCapital / ICT Support By Randy persoonsgegevens verwerkt in verband met deze website en SecurityAuditAI.

1. What we may process

  • Contact details you voluntarily provide, such as your name and email address.
  • Repository URLs and scan-related information when you request a scan.
  • Payment and subscription information processed by our payment provider.
  • Basic technical information needed to operate and secure the website.

2. Why we process it

We use information to provide requested scans, deliver reports, process subscriptions, respond to support requests, prevent abuse, maintain security and meet legal obligations.

3. Repository content

SecurityAuditAI may temporarily process repository content to perform a requested scan. The production system should be configured so temporary working data is deleted after processing. Logs, backups and third-party services must be reviewed separately before making an absolute “nothing is stored” promise.

4. Payments

Paid plans use Stripe checkout. Payment-card details are handled by Stripe rather than stored directly by CipherCapital.

5. Legal basis and rights

Depending on the situation, processing may be based on contract, legitimate interests, consent or a legal obligation. Under the GDPR/AVG you may have rights including access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. Neem contact op om toepasselijke rechten uit te oefenen.

6. Contact

E-mail: secrapports@outlook.com

For Dutch privacy complaints, you can also contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
