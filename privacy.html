Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026

Who is responsible

This policy covers CipherCapital and SecurityAuditAI at randysimpey.github.io/ciphercapital-hub and any domain pointing to it. Both are operated by ICT Support By Randy, a sole proprietorship based in Amsterdam, the Netherlands, registered for Dutch VAT.

What is collected

  • Repository URLs you submit. Used to clone the repository, run the scan, and produce your report. Nothing else.
  • Your email address, if you request a scan, ask for the checklist, or subscribe to a paid plan. Used to send you that report, that checklist, or account email.
  • Payment details, if you subscribe. These are collected and processed by Stripe directly — card details never reach us and are never stored on our side.
  • Basic analytics — pages visited, approximate region, device type — and only if you accept the analytics cookie in the banner.

What is not done

Your data is not sold. Your source code is not kept after a scan. There is no multi-step email automation and no drip sequence: checklist and tutorial emails go out by hand, when there's something worth sending.

Who else processes it

  • Stripe — payments for Pro and Business subscriptions.
  • Formspree — the checklist sign-up form.
  • Google Analytics — anonymised analytics, only after consent.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting for this site.

These providers may process data outside the EU/EEA under their own standard contractual clauses. Nothing is shared beyond what running the service requires.

How long it is kept

  • Cloned repositories — deleted as soon as the scan finishes, on every plan.
  • Scan report emails — kept only as long as support questions may follow, then deleted.
  • Checklist and subscriber addresses — kept until you unsubscribe or ask for removal.
  • Payment records — retained by Stripe under its own policy and the Dutch tax retention requirement, typically seven years.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can request access to, correction of, or deletion of the data held about you, object to processing, or ask for a copy. Email secrapports@outlook.com and you'll get a reply within a reasonable time. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Cookies

This site sets one analytics cookie, from Google Analytics, and only after you accept it in the banner. Declining limits nothing else on the site. You can change your mind by clearing this site's data in your browser, which brings the banner back.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: secrapports@outlook.com.
